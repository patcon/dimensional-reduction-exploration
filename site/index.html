MNIST, from pixels to projections

Each v1 version flattens MNIST digits into rows of pixels, clusters the rows scanpy-style (PCA → kNN graph → Leiden), and links the original and sorted pixel matrices to UMAP, PaCMAP and LocalMAP projections in 2D and 3D. Each builds on the one before. v2 turns to how PaCMAP gets there, running its optimisation live for you to play. v3 uses the digits as stand-in data for a valence visualisation. v4 lets people take part from their phones.

  1. v1.0Pixels to projections

    Full 28×28 greyscale digits: sorted matrices, linked projections, per-cluster samples and composition.

  2. v1.1Across resolutions

    Digits average-pooled from 32×32 down to 16×16, 8×8, 4×4 and 2×2, with projections recomputed at each and morphing between them.

  3. v1.2In black and white

    Every pooled digit binarised into stroke or background, so clustering and projections see only two levels, optionally drawn as agree / disagree votes.

  4. v2.0Playable PaCMAP

    PaCMAP's optimisation running live on up to all 65,000 digits, in 2D or 3D, with its neighbour, mid-near and far forces in your hands: attract or repel each from on-screen sliders or a MIDI controller, against PaCMAP's own schedule.

  5. v2.1Playable PaCMAP (mobile)

    Playable PaCMAP (mobile): building a mobile version of v2.0, to play PaCMAP's forces from a phone.

  6. v3.0Valence traces

    A valence visualisation with stand-in data: each digit's pixels, read in order, as one participant's valence over time, grouped by digit or Leiden cluster.

  7. v3.1Valence particles

    Valence particles, built on v3.0's stand-in data: each digit's pixels, read in order, as one participant's valence over time.

  8. v3.2Valence particles (mobile)

    Valence particles (mobile): building a mobile version of v3.1.

  9. v3.3Valence particles (speaker)

    Valence particles (speaker): comparing the MNIST digits against a ground truth of one digit broadcasting itself, the others heard by how far they agree.

  10. v3.4Valence particles (drawn paths)

    Valence particles (drawn paths): a fresh copy of v3.3, to read digits along their drawn paths.

  11. v4.0Valence participation

    Phones draw a digit by tilting, and appear as cursors among v3.3's digits. Open this on a phone, and /viz on the big screen.

  12. v4.1Valence music

    v4.0 with music: a random walk through the digits' nearest neighbours over the window, its chord set by their broad clusters and its melody by their small ones.
