MNIST, from pixels to projections

Each version flattens MNIST digits into rows of pixels, clusters the rows scanpy-style (PCA → kNN graph → Leiden), and links the original and sorted pixel matrices to UMAP, PaCMAP and LocalMAP projections in 2D and 3D. Each builds on the one before.

  1. v1Pixels to projections

    Full 28×28 greyscale digits: sorted matrices, linked projections, per-cluster samples and composition.

  2. v2Across resolutions

    Digits average-pooled from 32×32 down to 16×16, 8×8, 4×4 and 2×2, with projections recomputed at each and morphing between them.

  3. v3In black and white

    Every pooled digit binarised into stroke or background, so clustering and projections see only two levels, optionally drawn as agree / disagree votes.
